Trace showcase, ten real diagrams

Ten diagrams of the kind that end up in real docs, five of them networks with proxies, firewalls and zones. Each is drawn twice from its Trace source. Simple is what you write when you only list nodes, links and frames and let the layout do everything. Full adds the commands for when you want to decide: icons, rows and columns that place the zones, span and bridge nodes, and links written the way the traffic flows. The source of both is under each drawing. To edit one, paste it into the playground (dist/playground.html).
